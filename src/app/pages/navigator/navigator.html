Drag & drop navigator

A navigation rail with drag-and-drop reordering and grouping. The tree is remembered in this browser.

Try it

  • Drag an item near the top or bottom edge of another item to reorder.
  • Drop an item on the middle of another item to group them.
  • Hover over a collapsed group while dragging to expand it, then drop inside.
  • Drag items between groups, or out of a group into the root list.
  • A group left with one item is ungrouped automatically.
  • Open a group and drop an item on the middle of one of its items to put both in a menu.
  • Drop an item on the middle of a menu to add it, or hover to open the menu.
  • Drag items within an open menu to reorder them, or out of it into the rail.
  • A menu left with one item is replaced by that item.
  • Double-click a group, or press F2 on it, to edit its label and icon. Press F2 on a menu, or use "Edit menu" inside it, to edit the menu. Press Escape to cancel a drag.
  • On touch devices, press and hold an item to start dragging.
  • With the keyboard, focus an item, group or menu and press Alt+↑/↓ to move it, Alt+→ to move it into the group or menu above (or combine it with the item above), and Alt+← to move it out of its group or menu.
  • Use the menu button at the top of the navigator to expand or minimize it.
