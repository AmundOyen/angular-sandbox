Try it

  • Drag an item near the top or bottom edge of another item to reorder.
  • Drop an item on the middle of another item to group them.
  • Hover over a collapsed group while dragging to expand it, then drop inside.
  • Drag items between groups, or out of a group into the root list.
  • A group left with one item is ungrouped automatically.
  • Double-click a group, or press F2 on it, to rename it. Press Escape to cancel a drag.
  • On touch devices, press and hold an item to start dragging.
  • Use the menu button at the top of the navigator to expand or minimize it.
